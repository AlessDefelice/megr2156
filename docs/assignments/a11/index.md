## Analyze
We Chose stepper motor 6627T923. This motor has a maximum rated torque capacity of 467 In-OZ which is approximately 3.3NM. This exceeds our minimum required torque of 2.4 NM. The motor has a fine low rpm control due to its stepper design and spins a maximum of 300 Rpm, well above our 32-rpm required minimum. The Chosen stepper also has a good torque-speed graph that can deliver the required torque well past triple our required speed.

Our motor has an efficiency rating of 90%. It has a rated voltage of 24VDC and a current of 2 Amps per phase, giving 4 amps of total draw.

[mount pdf.pdf](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/35310971/mount.pdf.pdf)

I analyzed the forces and required strength for a motor mount, then I chose a set of 47065T854 T-Slot framing mounts that include hardware and T-nuts to bolt securely to our frame. I then drilled 4 precision holes at the NEMA 23 bracket size to mount the motor. This provides a removable solution with no special machining to bolt to the T-slot frame. The available mounts are well within the safety margin of strength, providing an accessible solution.

[coupler pdf.pdf](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/35311020/coupler.pdf.pdf)

I then used the same method to acquire the minimum thickness for a coupler. The smallest straight coupler available easily fit our strength requirements so I selected the 
6412K11 straight shaft coupler.

I analyzed the available mounting solutions, and selected corner mounts for their ease of access. Our minimum thickness was only 0.01 of an inch, so the provided 47065T833 corner bracket was well within the required thickness margin.
