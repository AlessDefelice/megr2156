A3 – Parametric Modeling and FEA

Objective
The Objective is to parametrically design a circular aluminum bar that will satisfy the following specifications. We are limiting our axial deflection to 9 thousandths of an inch. We are also given a Load of from 300 to 500Lb, and a young's modulus of 8.5 to 11.5E^6 Psi. We are then tasked to pick a reasonable area for the bar. The deflection is set and since length and area inversely affect delta, I went with a smaller area so that my bar did not get too unreasonably long. Rearranging the delta= PL/AE equation, I chose a 1 inch diameter bar and then solved for the length.
