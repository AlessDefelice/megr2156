# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective
This week we are tasked with designing a simple pulley system, given a few basic constraints. I find as we move further through engineering courses I'm given less and less specific information and forced to apply real world knowledge to the task at hand. The pulleys given are a very simple design, but we lack the specific inputs to the system to make it easy, instead we use our engineering knowledge and the freedom to choose our system to effectively design it.

## Analyze
I began as with all engineering assignments by listing the restrictions of the problem. I then drew a relevant FBD and modeled algebraically for my solutions. This is where I ran into my first issues. I needed the Friction coefficient for the belt tension equation, and it was not given. I did some research based on the material interaction between my chosen pulley material and the specified polyurethane belt. I was Listed a range of .2-.25 and so I chose the dead center to model my system. However thinking about it now, I believe it would have been a safer bet to choose the lowest expected coefficient of 0.2.
