# A6 – Design for Strength And Stiffness II

## Objective
The objective of this assignment builds off our previous bracket design. We are tasked with parametrically modeling our design from last week based on either strength or stiffness. In my hand calculations for A05, I saw that the strength required was higher than the required stiffness given the implementation of my design. As such, I chose to design for the strength. We are then tasked with creating a drawing with the proper dimensions and tolerances.
